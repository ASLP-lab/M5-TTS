THE RESEARCH

Abstract

01

We present M5-TTS, a compact language-model-based text-to-speech system that unifies multilingual and Chinese multidialect synthesis, zero-shot voice cloning, speaker-specific multilingual generation, and efficient CPU streaming within a 50M-parameter language model. M5-TTS employs a 16-codebook speech representation with multi-token prediction for efficient speech generation. To improve synthesis quality, we introduce a Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) framework that integrates pause, word/character error rate (WER/CER), and speaker-similarity rewards. We further propose CER-guided token-level credit assignment, which concentrates optimization on localized pronunciation errors rather than uniformly penalizing entire sequences.

On Seed-ZH and Seed-EN, M5-TTS achieves a CER of 1.37% and a WER of 2.91%, respectively, outperforming MOSS-TTS-Nano with only half the language-model parameters. On four cores of an Intel Xeon Gold 6530, M5-TTS achieves a real-time factor of 0.653 and a first-chunk latency of 225 ms, demonstrating practical CPU-only streaming synthesis. Subjective listening experiments suggest improved naturalness and expressiveness over Kokoro-TTS and so on. We will release the complete training, post-training, and inference pipelines, together with checkpoints for zero-shot voice cloning and a specific speaker, to facilitate reproducible research and the development of on-device speech generation and embodied voice interaction.

FROM WAVEFORMS TO TOKENS

Speech tokenizer

02

A low-frame-rate, causal representation for streaming speech. M5-TTS adopts Qwen-TTS-Tokenizer-12Hz, encoding each frame into one semantic token and 15 complementary acoustic tokens.

01

Semantic + acoustic

One semantic codebook and 15 acoustic codebooks preserve complementary speech information.

02

Low frame rate

12.5 frames per second keeps the temporal sequence compact for efficient generation.

03

Causal reconstruction

No look-ahead in the encoder or decoder, enabling incremental waveform synthesis.

SMALL MODEL, STRUCTURED GENERATION

Model architecture

03

A temporal backbone Transformer models speech across frames, while a lightweight multi-token prediction (MTP) module refines the acoustic representation within each frame.

One frame, sixteen tokens. The backbone retains its attention cache across frames; the MTP cache resets for each new frame. This separates temporal modeling from within-frame acoustic refinement without expanding all 16 codebooks into separate backbone time steps.
